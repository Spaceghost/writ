# Policy-aware audit traceability

Proposed change: [add-policy-aware-audit](../changes/add-policy-aware-audit/specs/policy-aware-audit/spec.md).
This change is proposed. Section 1 (event construction and access) is modeled
in Bend. Sections 2 (durability and history verification) and 3 (integration
evidence) are not implemented. Nothing here marks a host adapter or an
acceptance scenario as implemented: no host calls the audit wrappers, stores
the log, installs grants or delivers exports. Every log, sink, label table,
grant, epoch, `now`, event id and principal in the tests is SYNTHETIC
MONITOR STATE standing in for a trusted host input.
[docs/AUDIT.md](../../docs/AUDIT.md) lists each one and who must supply it.

Implementation: [AuditEvent.bend](../../AuditEvent.bend) (public entry points
`audited_admit`, `audited_commit`, `releasable`, `event_streams`, `encode`,
`decode`, `field_allowlist`, `field_allowed`, the named constants, accessors
and equality helpers), [AuditQuery.bend](../../AuditQuery.bend) (`empty`,
`respond`, `revoke_read`, `query_format_version`, `max_page`,
`public_denial`, `first_failed_gate`, accessors and equality helpers) and
[AuditExport.bend](../../AuditExport.bend) (`empty`, `transmit`,
`revoke_export`, `export_format_version`, `view_encode`, `view_tags`,
`first_failed_gate`, accessors and equality helpers). The library calls
audited, unchanged: [Transfer.bend](../../Transfer.bend) (`admit`) and
[DataOS.bend](../../DataOS.bend) (`commit_from_capability`).
Executable cases: [AuditTests.bend](../../AuditTests.bend), run by
`bend Tests.bend`, with the recorded run in
[verification/policy-aware-audit.txt](../../verification/policy-aware-audit.txt)
(JavaScript and native CPU, `--gpu off`; not a GPU test). Laws:
[LAWS.bend](../../LAWS.bend), proved in [PROOF.bend](../../PROOF.bend) under
ordinary `bend PROOF.bend` (not kernel verified).

A named test below is an existing `PASS` label, quoted exactly. Every stored
event in a test log comes from `audited_admit` or `audited_commit`, never
from a hand-written event, except in the decoder tests. "Reviewed-only gap"
means the requirement, or the named part of it, has no execution here; the gap
is stated in the requirement's own words.

## Model details

The wrappers are the only producers of a stored event. `audited_admit` calls
`Transfer.admit` exactly once and `audited_commit` calls
`DataOS.commit_from_capability` exactly once; `AuditQuery.bend` and
`AuditExport.bend` call neither, and nothing in the three modules calls the
backplane's commit directly or builds a WriteIntent.

Queries and exports read the log only through the visible view (`view`,
`export_log_view`). That is the modules' structure, shown by the tests; the
laws are stated over the view.

Not exercised anywhere in this change: durable append, crash injection and
recovery, history verification, transport authentication, any native host
(Genode, Unikraft, Linux, Windows, rclone), and the GPU.

Open questions put to Josh and Spaceghost: whether section 1 may land ahead
of add-durable-publication (ruled yes, because it appends nothing); who is the
authority for event-read and export grants (ruled: the monitor installs them,
recorded as the gap above); and whether visible-only metric counts are
acceptable to operators (ruled yes).
