Shim review 9 of 9: queue, take, put, and the transport refusal

This is the data path. A client writes a 64-byte record (16 words) after a 16-byte header into its request bucket, then signals. The writ_channel entrypoint queues one entry per channel in an 8-slot ring (1503-1519) and wakes a semaphore. writ_shim_take runs on a Bend runtime helper thread. It blocks on that semaphore, pops one entry, copies the record out once, and returns the record, the channel id, and the route subject stored at session creation. writ_shim_put copies Bend's reply into the result bucket without reading it and signals the client. One result is written by the shim itself: if the header length is not exactly 64, a fixed refusal record. GB-006's own scenario requires that ("dropped, counted, and answered with a fixed transport refusal before any Bend parsing"). See INVENTORY.md:87-88, :201-211, and the channel bounds at :409-455.

Open: writ_mem.cc:1334-1400, writ_mem.cc:1478-1519, writ_mem.cc:1669-1842
